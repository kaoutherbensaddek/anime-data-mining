4. Genre Explosion & Analysis (very high value for content-based recs)
This is almost mandatory for anime datasets.
Python# Split and explode genres
anime_df['genre_list'] = anime_df['genre'].str.split(', ')
exploded = anime_df.explode('genre_list')
exploded['genre_list'] = exploded['genre_list'].str.strip()

# Now you can groupby genre_list and calculate mean rating, count, etc.
genre_stats = exploded.groupby('genre_list').agg(
    count=('anime_id','size'),
    avg_rating=('rating','mean'),
    avg_members=('members','mean')
).sort_values('count', ascending=False)

print(genre_stats.head(15))
genre_stats['avg_rating'].sort_values().plot(kind='barh', figsize=(10,12))

Ratings are left-skewed → maybe use RMSE/MAE, not just accuracy
Huge long-tail in members → popularity bias is strong (SVD / collaborative filtering will love this)
